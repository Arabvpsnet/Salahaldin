## TV npm
- `sharp` reported **high**; upgrading within the old constrained range did not automatically resolve the installed advisory. Evaluate a compatible patch or supported major and rebuild the screenshot pipeline.
- `vite-plugin-pwa`, `workbox-build`, `minimatch`, `glob` reported moderate.
- Some advisories arise from transitive development dependencies; audit production and full-lock exposure separately.

## Server npm
- `form-data` reported **critical** (transitive).
- `axios`, `vite`, `vue`, `vue-i18n`, `lodash`, `rollup`, `postcss`, `nanoid` and other transitive packages reported high.
- `moment`, `@vueup/vue-quill`, `quill`, `esbuild` and `follow-redirects` also have reported advisories.
- Old Vite and Laravel/Vue tooling require a compatibility-tested upgrade, not blind package.json version editing.

## Composer PHP
- 52 advisory records distributed among: `guzzlehttp/guzzle`, `guzzlehttp/psr7`, `laravel/framework`, `league/commonmark`, `league/flysystem`, `symfony/http-foundation`, `symfony/http-kernel`, `symfony/mailer`, `symfony/mime`, `symfony/polyfill-intl-idn`, `symfony/routing`, `symfony/yaml`.
- Laravel 10 ended security support 2025-02-04 and should be treated as a **release blocker**, even if latest Laravel 10 patches address some individual CVEs.
- The advisory severity `None` means unspecified by the feed, not automatically safe.
- Do not sum npm vulnerability counts with Composer advisory records as though they were equivalent objects.
