# Salahaldin branding and third-party rights

The public product name is **Salahaldin — صلاح الدين**. Old brand names can be replaced in the visible title, splash, app navigation, settings labels and package identifiers after necessary migration checks. The upstream copyrights and MIT/GPL/AGPL clauses are independent of the UI brand and **must not be deleted**. No upstream trademark should be re-used as the Salahaldin logo. Pictures, QuranEnc translations, tafsir, audio and fonts have separate credit and reuse requirements.

Source control retains upstream LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES files. A compact legal-notices screen may be used instead of a large third-party credit banner. Do not claim translations/tafsir are authored by Salahaldin, or strip publisher metadata from the mushaf reader.
